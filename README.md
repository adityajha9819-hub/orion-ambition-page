# Orion Launch

Build a single premium product landing page for SARKAR ORION — a luxury unisex 

parfum. This is a redesign of an existing Shopify product page: keep the same 

product info below, but make the design more cinematic and high-end.

PRODUCT

- Name: Orion (100ml)

- Tagline: "Infinite. Cold. Ambitious."

- Scent profile: Fresh, Citrus, Aromatic

- Price: ₹1,499 (Incl. of all taxes)

- Category: Unisex Parfum

DESIGN DIRECTION

- Dark, editorial luxury aesthetic: near-black background (#0d0b0f), high-contrast 

  typography, generous negative space.

- Typography: refined serif/display serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for body 

  and UI text.

- Accent mood for Orion: cold blue/silver tones — used sparingly in image overlays, 

  glows, and small accent details, against the dark base.

- Subtle scroll-triggered fades and parallax on hero/gallery. Smooth hover states. 

  No gimmicky animation.

- Fully responsive, mobile-first.

PAGE STRUCTURE

1. Minimal sticky header — "SARKAR" logo, back-to-shop link, cart icon.

2. Hero: full-bleed bottle shot on left/center, product name "ORION" large serif, 

   tagline "Infinite. Cold. Ambitious." beneath it, price, "Add to Cart" button.

3. Gallery: 2-3 additional shots (packaging, lifestyle/mood shot) — sticky gallery 

   that stays in view while text scrolls beside it.

4. Scent notes breakdown: Top / Heart / Base notes, presented as an elegant 

   vertical or radial diagram (infer notes fitting "Fresh, Citrus, Aromatic").

5. Editorial paragraph: short brand-voice copy on the mood and inspiration behind 

   Orion — cold, ambitious, infinite — 2-3 sentences.

6. Details bar: 100ml size, longevity guidance, occasion guidance (e.g. daily wear, 

   versatile), "Unisex" callout.

7. Sticky "Add to Cart" bar that appears on scroll with price + button.

8. Cross-sell strip at the bottom: "Explore the rest of the collection" — small 

   cards for Noble, Throne, Regal with name + one-line scent profile, linking out.

9. Footer: minimal — policy links, contact email, social icons, copyright.

FUNCTIONALITY

- Add to Cart button (cart drawer or simple state, your choice) showing item, 

  price, and a checkout button.

- Sticky add-to-cart bar on scroll.

- Image gallery with smooth transitions between shots.

Keep the product name, tagline, price, and scent profile exactly as listed. Focus 

creative effort on layout, typography, imagery treatment, and motion — this should 

feel like a single flagship product page from a high-end fragrance house, not a 

generic Shopify template.
